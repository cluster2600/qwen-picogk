# AM C# training study — 14 October 2026

This separate study trains Qwen to write complete C# programs with PicoGK 2.3 for synthetic scan ingestion, morphological repair, bionic infill and additive-manufacturing geometry. **No AM C# adapter is qualified yet.** Eight completed courses are rejected: three 1.5B courses, three 7B courses and two 14B courses. The repository's published multilingual JSON planner and its defaults are preserved.

## Data and scientific scope

- [Original 150-pair ChatML JSONL](data/train.jsonl) is byte-identical to the requested dataset; [messages](data/train-chat.jsonl) preserve its user/assistant content for assistant-only loss. Categories are 45 scan, 60 infill, 30 DfAM and 15 debugging pairs. All 150 teachers compiled and completed native execution; only 12 geometric candidates passed their screens, while 138 were rejected. These are teaching/rejection examples, not 150 approved metal parts.
- [Corrective 64](data/corrective-001/train.jsonl) contains four fresh parameter/input variants of 16 families. Independent compilation, native execution and 900 Boolean checks pass. It includes 56 geometric coupons and eight bounded refusals, with actual input parsing, alternate fixtures, powder-port connectivity, shell cavities and TPMS family/width checks.
- [Header augmentation](data/corrective-002/train.jsonl) presents those unchanged 64 teachers under three generic headers: 192 rows and 55 distinct C# sources, not 192 new programs. The common development header stops before the task suffix; no development task body or reserved final content was copied.
- [Mixed 7B corpus](data/mixed-7b-001/train.jsonl) is the exact concatenation of original 150 messages and the 192 augmented rows. Actual 7B tokenizer and installed MLX dataset-mask checks cover all 342 rows; maximum sequence length is 5,416, under 6,144. No example is truncated.
- [Corrective 003](data/corrective-003/README.md) adds 16 fresh programs in 32 rows: eight prompt-only refusals and eight beams with specified radii and flat caps. Its retained native receipt passes all 16 programs and 648 Boolean checks; two deliberately incorrect beam variants are rejected. The first validation attempt is preserved with the correction for changing VDB file identifiers. Teacher source bytes are unchanged.
- [Targeted 224-row corpus](data/targeted-7b-001/train.jsonl) concatenates the 192 augmented rows and these 32 new rows exactly. Its actual 7B tokenizer receipt confirms complete assistant EOS, matching prompt prefixes and no truncation. [Course 005 registration](evidence/course-005-protocol.json) fixed a continuation from the rejected course 004 adapter for 448 steps over two epochs. The [completed CPU audit](evidence/course-005-cpu-audit.json) binds the finite arrays, exact parent/base/data/source/configuration identities and training receipt; it is not a model qualification.
- [Task-body diversity 004](data/corrective-004/README.md) uses four newly worded requests for each of the existing 80 validated teacher cases: 320 rows backed by 71 source programs. Assistant answers, C# sources and synthetic fixtures are unchanged. Independent semantic/source reviews and actual 7B tokenization pass, with a maximum of 2,181 tokens and no truncation. The first prose attempt is retained: sixteen normal-reader prompts were corrected to state the existing teacher's six-plane input guards, while all 320 answers remain unchanged. Complete author, manifest, history and review files are in Git; existing native geometry remains in its original evidence archives.
- [Short task-first 005](data/corrective-005/README.md) presents those same 80 teachers twice with 160 distinct short requests and unchanged answers, code, fixtures and native proof bindings. Bodies have median 503 and maximum 720 characters. Independent [semantic/source review](evidence/corrective005-independent-semantic-source-review.json) and actual 14B tokenizer/masking checks pass; maximum sequence length is 1,759, with exact EOS and no truncation. Complete text/history/row receipts are in Git, including corrected reviewer assumptions about receiver identifiers, varied report keys and distributed refusal wording. No teacher or authored corpus was changed by that audit; existing native proofs are reused.
- [Mixed 14B coverage corpus](data/mixed-14b-001/README.md) is the exact byte concatenation of the original 150 messages and the accepted 160 short rows: 310 rows. Independent CPU checks round-trip all original ChatML/content hashes and bind the supplemental answers, code, fixtures and existing native results. Actual 14B prompt masks/EOS/full fences pass, with a maximum of 5,416/6,144 tokens and no truncation. This inclusion preserves the original 12 accepted geometry screens and 138 rejections; it does not grant new geometry credit.

[Course 006 registration](evidence/course-006-protocol.json) fixed a one-epoch, 320-step continuation from course 005 using the accepted diversity corpus and learning rate 1e-5. Its historical [running-protocol audit](evidence/course006-running-protocol-audit.json) verified the registration and configuration without reading active run weights or logs, loading a model, or using the GPU. The [completed CPU audit](evidence/course-006-cpu-audit.json) subsequently verified all 224 finite arrays, exact parent/base/data/source/configuration identities and the completed training receipt. Complete course 006 weights, logs, captures and rejection evidence are preserved in one archive. Regenerating task-body prompts requires restoring the prior teacher result JSON files from their existing archives; the retained author refuses to overwrite a populated corpus.

[Course 008 registration](evidence/course-008-protocol.json) prospectively fixed that one-epoch, 310-step continuation at learning rate 2e-6, five times lower than course 007. Its [immutable runtime protocol](evidence/course008-runtime-protocol.json) and historical [running audit](evidence/course008-running-protocol-audit.json) bind the installed exact mixture, parent, base and unchanged strict gates. Completed weights, logs, captures, grades and rejection are retained in the course 008 archive. Final contents remain sealed.

The real API types are `Voxels`, `Mesh`, `Lattice` and `System.Numerics.Vector3`; `VoxelMatrix` and `PolyMesh` are not PicoGK 2.3 types. The compact teachers produce one complete C# fence, own their headless Library lifetime, export the same solid to VDB/STL and remove exactly zero-area facets. Refusal programs do not initialize native geometry. Scans and dimensions are generated synthetic fixtures; no proprietary scan or measured vehicle interface is included.

[Scientific sources](original150/sources.json) distinguish the dimensionless Fourier nodal surfaces of von Schnering and Nesper from exact SDFs. TPMS carriers are re-distanced before millimetre offsets. Known nodal-ray width samples are local finite-resolution checks, not global wall guarantees or Sigmund structural optimization. Point normals/sphere splats do not reproduce full calibrated Curless–Levoy fusion. Conical angles and voxel void connectivity do not prove support-free LPBF or physical powder evacuation; wall assumptions do not solve Mercelis–Kruth residual stress. Strength, fatigue, fitment, distortion and machine/process qualification remain open.

## Portable source and reproduction boundaries

Python 3.12 and [the pinned requirements](../requirements.txt) are required for the MLX tools; native evaluation requires macOS arm64 and the declared .NET 9.0.317/PicoGK 2.3 runtime. This subtree contains public copies of training, capture, evaluators, qualification guards and the isolated CLI. Path relocation changes their byte hashes, recorded in [source export bindings](evidence/source-export-bindings.json). Historical receipts retain original hashes; sanitized copies are provenance views and are not fresh runtime qualification.

The default base is the existing nine-file 1.5B identity in [base-manifest.json](base-manifest.json), restored externally into the repository's ignored `model/` directory. [base-profile-7b.json](base-profile-7b.json) specifies the optional pinned 7B conversion and its contained `am-code/model7b/` directory. All nine files are verified before MLX loading; extra shards, mixed explicit/default identities and escaping paths fail closed. Base weights are fetched from their pinned upstream repositories; neither base is redistributed here.

[base-profile-14b.json](base-profile-14b.json) separately pins the [MLX-community 14B conversion](https://huggingface.co/mlx-community/Qwen2.5-Coder-14B-Instruct-4bit/tree/29efdbab55a161237ab1e432a3abaf6c7ae2b477) at revision `29efdbab55a161237ab1e432a3abaf6c7ae2b477`. The [anonymous download receipt](diagnostics/model14b-preparation/download-receipt.json) verifies ten upstream files, including two shards totalling 8,309,494,233 bytes. The unchanged [repacker](repack_model14b.py) copies raw bytes into one nine-file runtime layout: it preserves all 1,255 tensor names, dtypes, shapes and payload hashes and only remaps index filenames. [Full tensor proof](diagnostics/model14b-preparation/repack-receipt.json) and [independent review](diagnostics/model14b-preparation/independent-review.json) bind the resulting profile and weight hash. No base payload is redistributed, converted or dequantized; these are identity proofs, not model qualification.

To restore it, use a fresh scratch root containing the exact repacker and the published download receipt at `diagnostics/model14b-preparation/download-receipt.json`. Fetch the ten named upstream files anonymously at the pinned revision into scratch `model14b-upstream/` and verify every size/SHA-256 from the receipt. Leave scratch `model14b/`, `base-profile-14b.json` and `diagnostics/model14b-preparation/repack-receipt.json` absent, then run the helper there. It deliberately refuses existing output/profile/proof files. Compare all nine generated profile hashes to the published profile and install only those nine verified runtime files into an absent, ignored `am-code/model14b/` directory. Preserve the published profile/proofs. Do not run the helper over this populated publication tree. Existing callers accept the optional `--base-profile am-code/base-profile-14b.json`; original defaults and serving authorization remain unchanged.

[base-profile-32b.json](base-profile-32b.json) pins the separate [MLX-community 32B conversion](https://huggingface.co/mlx-community/Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct-4bit/tree/d1e3b690c8e225d7795bccddf971ca6be68b2012). Its [prospective preparation plan](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/preparation-plan.json), anonymous [download receipt](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/download-receipt.json), [full raw-tensor proof](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/repack-receipt.json) and [independent review](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/independent-review.json) bind all 1,671 tensor names, dtypes, shapes and payloads. The [32B helper](repack_model32b.py) changes only repository/revision, four shard identities and isolated paths from the frozen 14B helper; its byte-copy and validation logic and eleven self-checks are unchanged. The independent review passes 2,197 checks. No base payload is redistributed or dequantized.

Twelve upstream files were verified. After independent review, explicit cleanup removed only the four newly downloaded shard intermediates, totalling 18,431,478,459 bytes. Eight small upstream files, [four exact source headers](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/source-headers/), the nine-file runtime and all proofs remain. The historical repack receipt records its pre-cleanup state; the append-only [cleanup plan](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/cleanup-plan.json) and [receipt](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/cleanup-receipt.json) record the later removals. Headers and hashes cannot reconstruct weights. Restore the pinned twelve upstream files in a fresh scratch root containing the exact 32B helper and download receipt at its expected relative path; leave its output, profile and repack receipt absent. Verify all upstream identities, run the helper, compare every generated runtime hash with the published nine-file profile, then install only those nine verified files into an absent ignored `am-code/model32b/`. Preserve the published proofs/profile and all older model files.

The actual [32B tokenizer audit](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/mixed310-tokenization-receipt.json) verifies the unchanged 310 rows, exact training/inference prefixes, assistant masks, EOS and complete fences; maximum length is 5,416/6,144 with no truncation. The historical [memory-smoke input preparation](diagnostics/model32b-preparation/memory-smoke-longest/provenance.json) preserves exactly the longest existing training row, original row 135. Its later [completed CPU audit](evidence/memory-smoke32b/cpu-audit.json) verifies one fresh training iteration, 224 finite FP32 arrays and 16,777,216 parameters. MLX reported a peak of 31,790,475,366 bytes (29.61 GiB), below the unchanged 36 GiB cap, for the actual 5,416-token sequence. This is one observed iteration; full-course stability and an actual 6,144-token training sequence remain unproven. The smoke adapter is ineligible for qualification and cannot be resumed by the full course. Its single final tensor file, configuration, exact input, logs and receipts are retained in the combined archive; no intermediate checkpoints were created.

The [untouched 32B baseline registration](evidence/baseline-32b-protocol.json) fixed temperature zero and 6,144 tokens before adaptation. All twelve [captured responses](evidence/baseline32b/capture/responses.json) reached EOS. The immutable [v1 grade](evidence/baseline32b/v1-report.json) and separate [v2 regrade](evidence/baseline32b/v2-report.json) both pass 5/12 with the same task set. Regrading queried no model and changed no captured response. The cone response passes the corrected lexical policy but still fails real C# compilation.

The prospective v2 checker uses [Roslyn lexical inspection](evaluation/PolicyLexer/Program.cs) to distinguish executable identifiers from harmless words in comments and messages, while preserving unsafe-operation and literal-path rejection. Its [tests and implementation receipts](diagnostics/evaluation-policy-v2/) preserve the independent eighteen-case lexical fixture and fourteen source/profile/scorer tests. Geometry oracles and the exact fifteen development and thirteen final qualification checks remain unchanged. The new [selection](qualification_v2.py), [final guard](final_qualification_v2.py) and [capture](infer_v2.py) bind one verified checker identity across matched baseline, candidate, scan and final evidence, including individual task records. Metadata aliases, including hardlinks to sealed final inputs, fail before access.

The path-redacted [checker profile](evidence/evaluation-policy-v2/checker-profile-v2.json) and [original SHA anchor](evidence/evaluation-policy-v2/checker-profile-v2.sha256) are historical provenance snapshots. The anchor intentionally retains the original private profile hash; it is not re-anchored to the exported JSON or portable public sources. Neither file is installed at the callable tools' default trusted-profile location. The [sealed reference manifest](sealed-reference-hashes-v2.json) contains only blind checksums; actual final prompts, reference programs and gold geometry remain withheld. Native/compiler binaries are also excluded. Public copies of the v2 policy accept the existing native-path environment variables; all original/public source differences are recorded in the ledger. Rebuilding the helper or relocating paths does not establish a new trusted checker profile or a qualified serving profile.

[Course 009 registration](evidence/course-009-protocol.json) fixes a fresh untouched 32B run over the exact original 150 plus 160 short rows, one epoch and 310 planned steps, at learning rate 5e-7. Its [immutable runtime protocol](evidence/course009-runtime-protocol.json) binds the installed mixture and configuration. The later [independent launch audit](evidence/course009-launch-independent-review.json), retained separately from the frozen combined archive, passes 32 grouped checks without reading active run telemetry or weights. It resumes no adapter, including the memory-smoke adapter, and starts a fresh optimizer. This snapshot records the launched run only; active weights, logs, captures and candidate scores are excluded. The same frozen v2 checker and unchanged independent gates will determine any later selection. No course 009 result or full-course memory claim is made here.

Configure `QWEN_PICOGK_AM_DOTNET` and `QWEN_PICOGK_AM_PICO` to externally restored native paths and verify their identities with `python am-code/check_publication.py --native-runtime`. [Native dependency metadata](native-runtime-manifest.json) provides exact hashes and upstream URLs. Candidate execution retains the macOS sandbox. Restoring geometry/reference evidence from release archives or validating development references is required before native candidate scoring. Public scan bindings use the repository's retained planner/selector files; their public-location freeze is distinct from the historical local freeze. Relocation therefore needs configuration and validation, rather than a claim of drop-in reproduction.

For data/source checks without MLX or native execution:

```sh
make check PYTHON=python3.12
python3.12 am-code/check_publication.py
```

`train.py` supports an exact-hash supplemental corpus, a fresh output run and an optional pinned `--base-profile`; failed adapters are retained. `infer.py` captures model text and never executes it. Development selection requires at least 11/12, all four critical families, all four scan tasks, no lost baseline pass, complete stopped responses and bound native/source/model evidence. Final acceptance requires at least 11/12 under its independently registered gates. Reserved final prompts, references, gold exports and their task author are withheld until actual model evaluation opens that boundary. The final guard source and acceptance contract are included as prospective metadata, without final content.

The isolated `qwen-picogk-am` CLI is disabled (`TRUSTED_PROFILE_SHA256=None`) until a complete qualified profile/receipt is deliberately installed. No failed checkpoint is promoted or made the default. The header-style explanation for earlier format failures remains a hypothesis; the observed 2/12 course still fails qualification.
